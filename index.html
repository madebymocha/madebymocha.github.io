(About me)

Hi, I'm Ogechi

I'm the creator behind Made by Mocha, based in LA's San Fernando Valley. I make content for travelers, book lovers, and anyone building a healthier routine.

I'll admit I'm a little "no niche, just vibes," and I've learned not to shy away from that. In my experience, content works when it's honest and relatable, whatever the topic, so that's what I focus on in everything I make.

I'm also getting married in spring 2027, so if you're a wedding or bridal brand, I'd love to hear from you.

I spent nine years in clinical research, so I explain products clearly and accurately and I care about the details. My videos feel like a recommendation from a friend who did her homework.
